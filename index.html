Preflight briefing — N1SFO

You fly the whole world

Home field is San Francisco. People are waiting in terminals everywhere, each one trying to reach somewhere else. Fly them there and get paid; the chart shows exactly where everyone is going.

  • Board passengers from the Terminal list, then click any field on the chart to plan the leg.
  • You can carry 14 seats and stop anywhere — pick up more people along the way.
  • Watch fuel: roughly 7,500 nm on full tanks. The longest hauls need a stop.
  • Deliver before a passenger's due time for full fare and better standing.
  • Speed time up with 60× or 300× while you're enroute.
