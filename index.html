How to play

  • Build from the sidebar: click an icon, wait for it to finish, then click the map to place it near your other buildings. Right-click an icon to cancel.
  • Windmills make grain. Run short and production halves, powered towers and the minimap go dark.
  • Mint + Ox Carts turn gold fields into coin — your only resource.
  • Advance the age to unlock stronger troops, engines and the Skyfire Battery.
  • Mouse: left-click or drag to select, right-click to move / attack / gather / set rally. Wheel zooms, arrow keys or screen edges scroll.
  • Touch: tap to select and to order, drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, long-press an icon to cancel.
  • Keys: Q W E R tabs · A attack-move · S stop · D deploy wagon · K repair · Z sell · H home · Space last alert · Ctrl+1…9 groups · P pause
