## Greenprint City

`greenprint/index.html` is a single-file browser game about planning a city for 2050.
Open it in any modern browser; it needs no build step or server.

Pick a city (San Francisco, Phoenix, Oslo or Singapore), then work through four chapters:

- **Energy**: split your power mix with drag handles, then rank grid storage upgrades.
- **Buildings**: zone your land and set the green-space share, then balance housing and commercial uses on semicircle sliders.
- **Transport**: rank transportation technologies, then divide street space among sidewalks, bike lanes, car lanes, bus lanes and rail.
- **Greenspace**: split green land among parks, habitat and farms, then set tree canopy and green-roof coverage.

An illustrated city scene changes with every choice. A live report scores climate, affordability,
livability and resilience, and the final report grades your plan against the city's starting point.
The scoring is a simplified model meant to show trade-offs, not to predict a real city.
