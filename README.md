# Solano Heat

An original open-world crime/driving sandbox that runs in the browser with three.js (WebGL2), tuned for iPhone (17 Pro class) with touch controls.

Sunset coastal city: 144 city blocks with two skyline clusters, a wide west-facing beach, a 14 m elevated freeway looping the whole city on pillars (with ramps down to the port and the airport), Solano International airport (runway, terminal, jet bridges, an airliner that takes off, circles the city and lands), the Port of Solano (container yard, gantry cranes, moored cargo ships, piers, boats, lighthouse), walk-in shops (24/7 Mart, Threads, and Mercado Fresco grocery stores across the city and on every island), restrooms in every shop and on both airport floors, a two-floor home (villa with pool: kitchen with a stove and fridge, dining room, living room with a TV and a guest bathroom downstairs; stairs up to the bedroom, a bathroom with a shower and a lounge), cooking (buy ingredients at the grocery, cook six recipes at home, then sit at the dining table to eat them for health and a 'well fed' boost; shoppers use the restrooms too), a family who lives with you and comes along when you go on a family trip (each with their own suitcase; they follow you on foot, ride in your car, come into shops, fly with you to the islands, sit next to you and share your Ferris wheel gondola; at home they walk around both floors, join you for dinner and movie night, sleep in their own beds and can be hugged), suitcases you can take on trips and check in at the airport, a rideable Ferris wheel at Bayside, city buses on a loop line with 8 bus shelters, 4 taxi stands with taxi rides anywhere in the city, a phone with an App Shop (travel apps FlyNow, BusGo and Cabby to fly, ride and call taxis from anywhere, paying for every trip; three original games; Bank, Weather, Maps; delete and re-download apps), Coastline Motors car dealer with 100 original cars for sale (sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs, pickups, sports cars, new and used), Fort Halcón (an off-limits military base north of the city, reached by a causeway: fenced with watchtowers and sweeping searchlights, a guarded gate that never opens, hangars, fighter jets, helicopters, tanks, trucks, barracks, a radar and a control tower; anyone without a pass who gets inside sets off the alarm, gets 3 stars and is escorted out; enlist at the guard booth to get army fatigues and a pass, and the gate opens for you: drive the army pickups and take a helicopter tour over the city from the helipad; paid duties at the duty board with promotions from Private to Captain), parking lots with cars to take, five island destinations reachable by plane from their own gates (San Marco B1, Port Coral B3, Santa Luz B4, Isla Verde B5, Bayside B6), each with an airstrip that flies home or on to the next island, plus a 'Surprise me' ticket, a two-floor airport (ground: check-in and security; escalators up to a mezzanine café and a 90 m glass concourse with moving walkways and gates B1–B6) where you buy boarding passes and fly, traffic on the streets and the freeway, pedestrians, police pursuits with on-ramp chasing, delivery jobs, cash pickups and stunt ramps.

## Saving

The game saves every few seconds, when you pause and when you switch away: where you are (on foot, in which car, or inside a shop or your house), the time of day, health, cash, route, bus passes, family trip, bank history, plus everything else (outfits, cars, family, phone apps, groceries, army rank, high scores). A copy is kept on the device and, on claude.ai, in your account's private save for this artifact (the newer one wins), so the game comes back even on another device. Rides in progress resume at their destination; police heat and timed jobs start fresh. New game (title screen or pause menu, tap twice) erases the save.

## Graphics

- PBR materials with image-based lighting from a physically based sky, ACES tone mapping
- Dynamic day/night cycle (16 min per day), moon, stars, night sky dome
- 4096 px sun/moon shadows that follow the camera (texel-snapped)
- Detailed original people: faces (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, ears), seven hair styles plus beards, caps and helmets, shaped torsos and builds, tees, long sleeves, tanks, open jackets and hoodies, belts, two-tone sneakers and backpacks, with a light low-detail version for distant crowds
- Lit shop windows with store interiors, OPEN neon, sidewalk signs and planters; airport ground crew vehicles at every gate, a drop-off canopy with taxis and blue taxiway lights; sandbags, jersey barriers, camo nets, tents and supply crates at Fort Halcón
- Working turn signals on every car (front corners, mirror repeaters, rear corners) with a tick-tock sound and dashboard arrows; traffic signals before turning at intersections
- Detailed original car models: sculpted bodies with wheel arches, tinted glass with a painted roof, LED headlight strips and full-width tail-light bars, grille, mirrors, door lines, chrome trim, plates, diffuser and exhaust tips, 5-spoke rims on rounded tyres (sports cars get dark rims and a wing, SUVs roof rails), with a matching low-detail version for distant cars
- HDR bloom, 4x MSAA, clear-coat car paint, procedural glass curtain walls
- Ocean with analytic shoreline depth, multi-wave normals, foam
- Wind-swayed palm fronds, tire smoke, street-light pools at night
- Quality presets (Ultra / High / Balanced) plus automatic resolution scaling to hold 60 fps
