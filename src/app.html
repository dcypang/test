    Preflight briefing — N1SFO

    You fly the whole world

    Home field is San Francisco. People are waiting in terminals everywhere, each one trying to reach somewhere else. Fly them there and get paid — hand the leg to dispatch and watch it run on the chart, or climb in and fly it yourself.

    • Board passengers from the Terminal list, then click any field on the chart to plan the leg.
    • You can carry 14 seats and stop anywhere — pick up more people along the way.
    • Watch fuel: roughly 7,500 nm on full tanks. The longest hauls need a stop.
    • Deliver before a passenger's due time for full fare and better standing.
    • Speed time up with 60× or 300× while you're enroute.
    • Looking for somewhere specific? Type a code or city into Direct to, or press /.
    • Want to fly it yourself? Hit Cockpit (or C): thrust with W, rotate at 145 kt, steer with the arrow keys, and A engages the autopilot.
